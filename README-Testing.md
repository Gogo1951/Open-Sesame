# Open Sesame // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Open Sesame, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Open-Sesame/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Open-Sesame/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, Season of Discovery on the Classic Era client, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **A character on every flavor**, including one on a Season of Discovery realm. A run that skips one is not a finished run.
- **Lua errors switched on.** Install BugSack and !BugGrabber, or type `/console scriptErrors 1` once on each client. With neither, the game swallows Lua errors silently and a broken step can look like a pass.
- **A Rogue with Lockpicking trained**, on every flavor. Steps 9, 15, 16 and 23 need one. Any skill level works: a rank far below what a box asks for is as useful a result as a rank above it.
- **Openable containers.** Clams are the easiest (Small Barnacled Clam, Big-mouth Clam, Thick-shelled Clam), from fishing or murloc drops. Bring several. Auto-Opening opens them within seconds of reaching your bags, so keep your stock in the **bank**, where Open Sesame never looks, or left-click the mini-map button to switch Auto-Opening off while you carry them.
- **Locked boxes.** A way to loot a fresh one in step 15. A Junkbox from your Rogue's Pick Pocket is the easiest, or any Lockbox fished up or looted from humanoids.
- **Something to kill and loot repeatedly**, for coin, greens, and a common drop you can farm (cloth, a grey trinket). Humanoids give you all of it at once.
- **A Bind on Pickup drop you can get solo**, for step 7. An over-levelled character clearing the first boss of an early dungeon (The Deadmines, Wailing Caverns) is the quickest source.
- **Bag space you can fill and empty on demand**: vendor trash you're willing to carry, so you can drop to exactly four free slots, below four, to zero, and back out.
- **A vendor** you can walk to, for step 16.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional spot-check in step 23.

Unless a step says otherwise, be **out of combat, out of instances, ungrouped, and not stealthed**, with no vendor, mail, bank or trade window open. Every step in this plan works solo.

This plan deliberately skips the Where and Group hold-offs, the Pick Pocket sound, the Lockboxes panel's For All Characters setting, containers whose level is above yours, master loot and group rolls, the Profiles panel, and what the Display Context, API Endpoints and Library Versions reports say beyond producing readable text. Those are judged by eye or need a group; the steps below cover the paths that break silently.

## Verify this release's changes

This is Open Sesame's final release. It prints an **End of Support** notice at every login pointing players to GogoLoot, removes **Loot Toasts** and clears their saved settings, splits the add-on's item lists into one set per flavor (Season of Discovery now has its own), keeps the loot window up for **Bind on Pickup** items, guards WoW Forever's in-combat reads, changes how your **Lockpicking rank** is found, and rebuilds **Diagnostic Tools** into tabs. These nine steps are the ones that prove it.

**Loot Toasts removed**

**1.** At the character select screen, click **AddOns**. Open Sesame must be listed **exactly once**, ticked, and **not** marked out of date or incompatible. Log in. No Lua error may appear, and no panel headed **Open Sesame Loot Toasts** may appear anywhere on screen, even on a character that has never dismissed it. Failure is a Lua error naming Open Sesame, or the drag handle or any toast row showing up.

**2.** With Speedy Loot and Loot Sound on, kill a humanoid and loot it. The loot window must never appear, every item and the coin must land in your bags, and **no on-screen loot rows** may appear. An Uncommon (green) or better must still play a single chime. Then open **Options → AddOns → Open Sesame → Notifications**. Its description must read *"Speedy Loot hides the loot window, so these sounds tell you what you just picked up."*, and below it there must be only the **Loot Sound** section: **Enable Loot Sound**, the **Minimum Quality** dropdown with its speaker button, and **Enable Pick Pocket Sound**. Failure is a toast row on screen, any Loot Toasts control left in the panel, the word "toasts" in the description, or a Lua error as the loot lands.

**3.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, open the **Settings** tab, and click **Run** on the **Saved Variables** row. No key beginning with `lootToast` may appear anywhere in the output box, under any profile, and `global` must hold no `lootToastPosition`. This matters most on a client where you used Loot Toasts before updating. Failure is any `lootToast` key left in the report.

**End of Support notice**

**4.** Log in with **Enable Welcome Message** ticked, as it is by default. Directly beneath the welcome line, a second line must print:

> Open Sesame // End of Support: this add-on is now part of GogoLoot, and this is its final release. Install GogoLoot to keep getting updates, and then you can remove Open Sesame.

Type `/reload`: the line must print again, still directly beneath the welcome line. Failure is the line missing, the line printing above the welcome line, or `nil` or a raw key such as `CHAT_END_OF_SUPPORT` where the text belongs.

**5.** Type `/os`, untick **Enable Welcome Message**, and type `/reload`. The End of Support line must still print, now on its own, because it has no switch of its own. Tick the welcome message back on afterwards. Failure is the End of Support line vanishing along with the welcome line, or the welcome line still printing.

**One item list per flavor**

**6.** In **Diagnostic Tools**, open the **Data** tab. The silver line under each of its five rows (Openable-Items, Lockbox-Skill-Levels, Default-Ignore-Items, Spells, Game-IDs) must name a file in **this flavor's** folder: `Vanilla/` on Classic Era, `Discovery/` on Season of Discovery, `Camelot/` on WoW Forever, `TBC/` on TBC Anniversary. Click **Validate All Data Files** and wait for every row to finish; it can take a minute. The output box's first line must end in `Flavor Vanilla // Data Vanilla`, `Flavor Vanilla (Season of Discovery) // Data Discovery`, `Flavor Camelot // Data Camelot`, or `Flavor TBC // Data TBC` to match. Every row must read **Done** with an OK count. `INCOMPLETE` beside it is acceptable. Then open the **Ignore List** panel: every row must settle into an icon and an item name within a few seconds. **Season of Discovery is the flavor most likely to break here**, since its list is new this release. Failure is a row reading `NOT ON CLIENT` or `TABLE MISSING`, a Season of Discovery header that says `Data Vanilla`, or an Ignore List row stuck on *"Loading item ..."* or a bare number.

**Bind on Pickup loot**

**7.** With Speedy Loot and Loot Sound on, loot a corpse that holds a **Bind on Pickup** item. The game's warning that the item will bind to you must appear, with the loot window **still visible** behind it. Click **Okay**: the item must land in your bags, and if it is Uncommon (green) or better, the chime must play once. Everything else on the corpse must already be in your bags. Failure is the loot window vanishing with the item left on the corpse, the warning never appearing, or no chime for a green or better.

**WoW Forever in combat**

**8.** *WoW Forever only.* Carry a clam with Auto-Opening off. Pull a mob, and once you're in combat, left-click the mini-map button to turn Auto-Opening on. Fight the mob for at least fifteen seconds, using several of your abilities, and as a Rogue open from Stealth. No Lua error naming Open Sesame may appear at any point in the fight, and the clam must stay shut. Kill the mob and stand still: the clam must open on its own within a few seconds of combat ending. Failure is any Lua error during the fight, the clam opening mid-fight, or the clam still shut ten seconds after combat ends.

**Lockpicking rank**

**9.** On your Rogue, open **Diagnostic Tools → Settings** and click **Run** on the **Open Sesame Context** row. On **Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary**, the line *"Lockpicking rank as the add-on reads it:"* must end in a **number** that matches the Lockpicking rank on your character sheet's **Skills** tab. On **WoW Forever** it must read *"unavailable on this client (or not trained)"*, which is correct, since that client can't report the rank. Failure is the "unavailable" wording on Classic Era, Season of Discovery or TBC Anniversary, or a number that disagrees with the Skills tab.

When steps 1-9 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**10.** Log out to character select and back in. No Lua error window may appear and no red error text may print. A coloured welcome line must print: *"Open Sesame // Version ... Settings (including the option to disable this message) can be found under Options > AddOns > Open Sesame. Enjoying the add-on? Tell a friend about it! (="* It must show a real dated version in a packaged build, or **Dev** in an unpackaged working copy. If your Auto Loot setting was off, one extra line reads *"Auto Loot has been enabled. Open Sesame requires it to function."*, which is correct. Then type `/reload`: the UI must come back clean and the welcome line must print again. Failure is any error naming Open Sesame, a missing or uncoloured welcome line, or a line containing `nil` or a stray `%s`.

**11.** Open the settings three ways: type **`/os`** (the add-on's only slash command), **Shift + Middle-Click** the mini-map button, and press `Esc` → **Options** → **AddOns** → **Open Sesame**. All three must land you in the same place: the settings **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Open Sesame selected in the category list on the left and five entries beneath it in this order: **Notifications**, **Lockboxes**, **Ignore List**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools**. Failure is nothing happening, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a tester who ran only Classic Era has not finished this step.

**12.** Pull a mob, and while still in combat type **`/os`**, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button. Each must print *"As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat."* and the panel must **not** open. Kill the mob and stand still: the panel must not open by itself once combat ends. Failure is the panel opening, silence with no message, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Open Sesame.

**13.** Hover the mini-map button and read its three keybind lines, then try each. **Left-Click** must toggle Auto-Opening, **Right-Click** must toggle Speedy Loot, and **Middle-Click** must toggle Loot Sound. After each click the tooltip must redraw in place with that feature's state flipped between **Enabled** and **Disabled**. The button's icon must follow Auto-Opening: green bag on, red bag off, black bag paused. On **WoW Forever**, the bag must sit **centred and round** inside the button's ring. Failure is a click doing something other than what its line says, a state that doesn't update until you move the cursor away, an icon that disagrees with the tooltip, or a square icon hanging off-centre on WoW Forever.

**14.** With Auto-Opening on, put several clams in your bags. They must open one after another with no clicks from you, over a second or two. Now fill your bags until you have **fewer than four free slots**. Chat must read *"Auto-Opening is paused until you have at least 4 empty bag slots."*, the mini-map icon must go black, and nothing more may open. Free a slot so you have four: chat must read *"Auto-Opening has resumed."* and any clams still waiting must open. Failure is clams sitting unopened with slots to spare, clams opening while paused, or a pause message that never resolves into a resume.

**15.** On your Rogue, pickpocket or loot a fresh locked box. Chat must read *"[Box] will open automatically once it is unlocked."*, with the box as a clickable link. On a non-Rogue this line is deliberately silent unless **Lockboxes → Show for** is set to For All Characters. Hover the box. On **Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary** there must be a block headed **Open Sesame** below the game's own lines, carrying a Lockpicking number that is **green when your skill clears the box and red when it doesn't**. Which line you get depends on the client: where the game already prints its own *Requires Lockpicking* line, the block shows *Your Lockpicking* with your rank instead of repeating it, so expect flavors to differ. On **WoW Forever**, which can't report your rank, the requirement must be stated **exactly once**: either the game's own *Requires Lockpicking* line with no Open Sesame block under it, or an **Open Sesame** block with a **white** number, and never a *Your Lockpicking* line. Hover the mini-map button: a **Locked Items** heading must list the box, with ` x2` beside it if you carry two of the same. Now pick the lock and **move your cursor off the box**, since nothing opens while you're hovering an item. The box must open itself within a second or so, with no second click, and its row must be gone from Locked Items. Failure is no chat line, no block on a Rogue who plainly has the skill, both lines at once, a colour that disagrees with the numbers, or a box that sits unopened after it's unlocked and un-hovered.

**16.** Left-click the mini-map button to turn **Auto-Opening off**, put a clam in your bags, and talk to a vendor. With the vendor window open, left-click the button to turn Auto-Opening back **on**. The clam must stay shut. Close the vendor window: the clam must open on its own within a second or two. Repeat it twice more: once on your Rogue with **Stealth** instead of the vendor, and once with Speedy Loot switched off and a corpse's **loot window** left open. The clam must stay shut while you're stealthed or the loot window is up, and open within a second or two of leaving Stealth or closing the window. Switch Speedy Loot back on. Failure is a clam opening while any of those is active, which would break your Stealth or replace the corpse's loot, or a clam that stays shut once you're clear.

**17.** With Speedy Loot on and plenty of bag room, loot a corpse. The loot window must **never appear at all**, not even a flash, and everything, coin included, must land in your bags. Now fill your bags completely and loot a corpse holding real items: the loot window must **stay up** with those items still in it, so you can take them by hand. Failure is a loot window flashing for half a second on every corpse, or a window hidden while loot is still stranded in it.

**18.** Pick something common you can farm, such as cloth or a grey drop. Paste its item link or item ID into the **Add Item** box on the **Ignore List** panel and press Enter. It must appear as a row with its icon straight away. Kill something that drops it: Speedy Loot must **leave it in the loot window**, and chat must read *"[Item] is on your Ignore List, so Speedy Loot left it in the loot window."* Wait at least five seconds, then take it by hand: chat must now read *"[Item] is on your Ignore List, so Auto-Opening will leave it alone."* Next, with Auto-Opening off, put a clam in your bags, add the clam to the list too, and turn Auto-Opening back on. The clam must stay **shut**. Finally, click **Restore Defaults** and confirm the prompt. Both of your additions must be gone, and the clam must open on its own within a second or two. Failure is an ignored item looted anyway or left behind with no explanation, a clam opening while it's on the list, or a Restore Defaults that fires with no confirmation or leaves your additions behind.

**19.** With **Loot Sound** on, loot an Uncommon (green) or better off a corpse: a single chime must play. Loot only grey and white items off the next one: no chime. Open **Notifications**, set **Minimum Quality** to Rare and click the speaker beside it: the chime must play once. Set it back to Uncommon. Failure is no chime on a green off a corpse, a chime on grey or white loot, or a speaker button that stays silent.

**20.** Read back the add-on's chat lines you've now seen: the pause and resume lines (step 14), the box waiting to be unlocked (step 15), and the two Ignore List lines (step 18). Every one must be branded **Open Sesame //**, read as one complete sentence, and carry a **clickable item link** where an item is named. The count in the pause line must read as a real number. Nothing this add-on prints is ever sent to another player, so none of it may show up in any chat channel. Failure is `nil` or a stray `%s` or `%d` in a line, a raw `|cff...|Hitem:` sequence showing as text instead of a link, or the same line repeating over and over as your bags update.

**21.** Type `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools**. Only the warning paragraph and the **Enable Diagnostic Tools** toggle may be visible, and the toggle must be **off**, even though you left it on earlier, because diagnostics never carries over a reload. Tick it: four tabs must appear without reopening the panel, **Run Tests**, **Settings**, **Code** and **Data**. On **Run Tests**, click **Start** in the Event Log box. Fill your bags completely, open a corpse with Speedy Loot off, and click an item in the loot window so the game tells you your inventory is full. Then press an ability that's on cooldown a dozen times, so the game prints red errors that have nothing to do with looting. Come back, click **Stop**, then **Show**, and switch Speedy Loot back on. The inventory-full firing must appear as a **full timestamped line**. The cooldown errors must **not** appear as individual lines. Instead they must be one counted row in the block at the end headed **"Suppressed uncorrelated traffic:"**, in the shape `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE(56, Ability is not ready yet.) x12` (the number in brackets can differ by client). Failure is the toggle already on after a reload, cooldown spam flooding the log as individual lines, or the inventory-full firing swallowed into the summary.

**22.** On the **Settings** tab, click **Run All Settings Reports**. The output box must show the client line once, then four blocks headed `---- Open Sesame Context ----`, `---- Saved Variables ----`, `---- Display Context ----` and `---- Other Add-ons ----`, each full of readable text, and **Other Add-ons** must list `Open-Sesame` ending in `[loadable]`. On the **Code** tab, click **Run All Code Reports**: the **Event Registration** row must read **Done: all register**, and the box must fill with all three blocks. Untick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**: all four tabs must vanish at once. Failure is any block reading `ERROR:`, a blank box, Event Registration reporting failures, or tabs left behind after unticking.

**23.** *Optional, and only worth running on a non-English client.* Log in on one and open every panel. Every label, description, note and mouseover tooltip must render in that language. Failure is a raw key like `OPTIONS_ENABLE_LOOT_SOUNDS` or `LOCKBOXES_DESCRIPTION` showing on screen instead of words. Then trigger the pause message and an item message: the number and the item name must land inside sensible sentences, with no `nil` and no leftover `%s` or `%d`. Finally, on a Rogue with Lockpicking trained on Classic Era, Season of Discovery or TBC Anniversary, repeat step 9: the rank must be a **number**. Then hover a locked box: the Open Sesame block must be there, its number green or red. **This last check is the highest-risk one here.** The add-on finds your rank by the skill's name exactly as that client spells it, and when the match fails, there is no error to warn you. The number silently turns white, or on a flavor that prints its own requirement line, the block never appears at all.

When every step passes on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
