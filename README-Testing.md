# Open Sesame // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Open Sesame, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Open-Sesame/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Open-Sesame/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **All three clients**, each with a character on it. **WoW Forever is new this release** and has never been through this plan, so it is the flavor most likely to break. A run that skips it is not a finished run.
- **Lua errors switched on.** Install BugSack and !BugGrabber, or type `/console scriptErrors 1` once on each client. With neither, the game swallows Lua errors silently and a broken step can look like a pass.
- **A Rogue with Lockpicking trained**, on every flavor. Steps 1, 2, 6, 16, 17 and 23 need one. Any skill level works: a rank far below what a box asks for is as useful a result as a rank above it.
- **Openable containers.** Clams are the easiest (Small Barnacled Clam, Big-mouth Clam, Thick-shelled Clam), from fishing or murloc drops. Bring several. Auto-Opening opens them within seconds of reaching your bags, so keep your stock in the **bank**, where Open Sesame never looks, or left-click the mini-map button to switch Auto-Opening off while you carry them.
- **Locked boxes.** One in your bags from the start, kept locked through step 7, and a way to loot a fresh one in step 16. A Junkbox from your Rogue's Pick Pocket is the easiest, or any Lockbox fished up or looted from humanoids.
- **Something to kill and loot repeatedly**, for coin, greens, and a common drop you can farm (cloth, a grey trinket). Humanoids give you all of it at once. For step 8 you also need a spot where you can pull **two at once**.
- **Bag space you can fill and empty on demand**: vendor trash you're willing to carry, so you can drop to exactly four free slots, below four, and back out.
- **A vendor** you can walk to, for step 17.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional spot-check in step 23.

Unless a step says otherwise, be **out of combat, out of instances, and not stealthed**, with no vendor, mail, bank or trade window open. Every step in this plan works solo.

This plan deliberately skips the Loot Toasts appearance settings (font, size, outline, grow direction, alignment, seconds on screen, maximum rows, minimum quality and the nine Always Show toggles), the Where and Group hold-offs, the Pick Pocket sound, the Lockboxes panel's For All Characters setting, and what the Loot Method, Relevant CVars, Display Context, API Endpoints and Library Versions reports say beyond producing readable text. Those are judged by eye or untouched this release; the steps below cover the paths that break silently.

## Verify this release's changes

This release gives each flavor its own TOC and adds **WoW Forever** as a third. It also splits the container, Ignore List and Lockpicking data into per-flavor files and moves every item and spell lookup onto calls WoW Forever still has. Beyond that, it stops all opening work in combat, keeps Speedy Loot's window up when a pass leaves your bags tight, gives every options control a mouseover tooltip, and adds the Player & Spells, Locked Boxes and Validate Data reports to Diagnostic Tools. These ten steps are the ones that prove it.

**One TOC per flavor, and WoW Forever**

**1.** At the character select screen, click **AddOns**. Open Sesame must be listed **exactly once**, ticked, and **not** marked out of date or incompatible. On WoW Forever it must be there at all, which is new this release. Log in on your Rogue. On a client where Open Sesame has never run before (always the case on WoW Forever), a dark panel headed **Open Sesame Loot Toasts** must also appear above the centre of the screen, with *"Click + Drag to Position"*, *"Right-Click to Lock"*, a **Disable Loot Toasts** button and a stack of **Example Item** rows. Drag it to where you want loot to show, then right-click it away. Failure is Open Sesame missing from the list, listed twice or flagged out of date, or no introduction panel on a first login.

**2.** Open **Options → AddOns → Open Sesame → Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, and click **Read Player & Spells**. The first line must end with the flavor you're on: **Flavor Vanilla** on Classic Era (with **+ Season of Discovery** added on a Season of Discovery realm), **Flavor Forever** on WoW Forever, and **Flavor TBC** on TBC Anniversary. The last line gives your Lockpicking rank as the add-on reads it: a **number** on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, and *"unavailable on this client (or not trained)"* on WoW Forever, which can't report it. Failure is a header naming the wrong flavor or reading `nil`, or no rank on Classic Era or TBC Anniversary for a Rogue who has the skill.

**Data split by flavor**

**3.** Still in Diagnostic Tools, scroll down to the four **Validate Data** sections and click each button in turn: **Validate Data/Openable-Items.lua**, **Validate Data/Lockbox-Skill-Levels.lua**, **Validate Data/Data.lua** and **Validate Data/Default-Settings.lua**. Each box counts up (*"Validated 100 / 333 ..."*), then a report replaces it whose second line is a tally, such as `Data/Openable-Items.lua // 333 item ids: 333 OK, 0 NOT ON CLIENT, 0 NOT LOADED`. The id counts must be **333, 19, 4 and 27** on Classic Era and WoW Forever, and **404, 21, 4 and 32** on TBC Anniversary. Every tally must show **0 NOT ON CLIENT**. A few NOT LOADED on a first run means the server was slow to answer: click the button again and they must clear. Failure is any NOT ON CLIENT above 0, which is an item this client doesn't have, so copy the whole report into your bug report. TBC Anniversary's counts showing on Classic Era or WoW Forever, or a count that never finishes, is also a failure.

**4.** Open **Ignore List**, click **Restore Defaults** and confirm the prompt. On **TBC Anniversary** the list must include **Black Sack of Gems** and **Cache of the Shattered Sun**. On **Classic Era and WoW Forever** neither may appear, because those clients don't have them. On every flavor, every row must settle into an icon and a real item name within a few seconds. Failure is a TBC-only container listed on Classic Era or WoW Forever, one missing on TBC Anniversary, or a row stuck as a bare item number.

**WoW Forever's missing calls**

**5.** *WoW Forever only.* With plenty of bag room and Speedy Loot on, kill a humanoid and loot it. The loot window must never appear, every item and the coin must land in your bags, and each Loot Toast row must show **that item's own icon** beside its name. Then hover the mini-map button with your locked box in your bags: its **Locked Items** row must show the box's icon too. Failure is the loot window staying up with the items still in it, a toast or Locked Items row with a blank or question-mark icon, or a Lua error as the loot lands.

**6.** *WoW Forever only.* On your Rogue, hover the locked box. WoW Forever can't tell the add-on your Lockpicking rank, so the requirement must be stated **exactly once**. Either the game prints its own *Requires Lockpicking* line and no Open Sesame block appears under it, or an **Open Sesame** block appears with a **white** *Requires Lockpicking* number. A *Your Lockpicking* line never appears on this flavor. Failure is a Lua error on hover, the requirement shown twice, or no requirement anywhere on the tooltip.

**Locked Boxes report**

**7.** Left-click the mini-map button to turn **Auto-Opening off**, put a clam in your bags alongside the locked box, and in Diagnostic Tools click **Read Locked Boxes**. There must be a row for each bag slot holding a container: the locked box reading **LOCKED true**, the clam reading **OPENS_IMMEDIATELY true**, and **TOOLTIP_LINES** above 0 on every row. Left-click the button again to turn Auto-Opening back on: the clam must open and the box must stay put. Failure is the locked box reading LOCKED false, any row showing 0 TOOLTIP_LINES, or red error text about the lock once Auto-Opening is back on. Any of these means the add-on can't see the lock on this client and will keep trying to open boxes it can't.

**No opening work in combat**

**8.** *Most important on WoW Forever*, where the game hides aura and cast details from add-ons during combat, but run it on every flavor. Left-click the mini-map button to turn **Auto-Opening off**, put a clam in your bags, and pull **two** mobs. Once the fight has started, left-click the button again to turn Auto-Opening back **on**. Fight normally with your abilities, and press one that's on cooldown a few times so the game complains in red. Then kill the first mob and **loot it while the second is still hitting you**: the loot must land in your bags with no loot window. No Lua error may appear at any point, and the clam must stay **shut** for the whole fight. Kill the second mob: the clam must open on its own within a couple of seconds of combat ending. Failure is any Lua error naming Open Sesame, the clam opening mid-fight, or the clam still shut after combat ends.

**Speedy Loot keeps the window when bags are tight**

**9.** With Speedy Loot on, fill your bags until **exactly four** general slots are free (profession bags and quivers don't count). Kill something and loot a corpse with at least one item on it. Everything must land in your bags, but this time the loot window is **allowed to appear**: it must stay up until the last item is in your bags, then close by itself. You'll also see the Auto-Opening pause message, because you're now below four free slots; that's correct. Failure is the window vanishing while the corpse still sparkles with loot on it, an item missing from both your bags and the window, or a window that stays open with nothing left in it.

**Mouseover tooltips on every control**

**10.** Open each panel, **Open Sesame**, **Notifications**, **Lockboxes**, **Ignore List** and **Diagnostic Tools** (with the tools enabled), and rest the cursor on every checkbox, dropdown, slider and button. Each must show a tooltip naming the control, then one plain sentence saying what it does. Enable Auto-Opening, for example, reads *"Turns automatic opening of clams and unlocked containers on or off."* A few controls are exempt. The four Feedback & Support link boxes and the Diagnostic Tools report boxes are for copying text and carry no tooltip. Profiles is the standard panel every Ace add-on shares. An Ignore List row's tooltip is the item itself. Failure is any other control with no tooltip, a tooltip with only the control's name, or a raw key such as `OPTIONS_ENABLE_AUTO_OPENING_DESCRIPTION` in place of words.

When steps 1-10 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**11.** Log out to character select and back in. No Lua error window may appear and no red error text may print. A coloured welcome line must print, in the shape *"Open Sesame // Version ... Settings ... can be found under Options > AddOns > Open Sesame."* It must show a real dated version in a packaged build, or **Dev** in an unpackaged working copy. If your Auto Loot setting was off, one extra line reads *"Auto Loot has been enabled. Open Sesame requires it to function."*, which is correct. Then type `/reload`: the UI must come back clean and the welcome line must print again. Failure is any error naming Open Sesame, a missing or uncoloured welcome line, or a line containing `nil` or a stray `%s`.

**12.** Open the settings three ways: type **`/os`** (the add-on's only slash command), **Shift + Middle-Click** the mini-map button, and press `Esc` → **Options** → **AddOns** → **Open Sesame**. All three must land you in the same place: the settings **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Open Sesame selected in the category list on the left and five entries beneath it in this order: **Notifications**, **Lockboxes**, **Ignore List**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools**. Failure is nothing happening, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a tester who ran only Classic Era has not finished this step.

**13.** Pull a mob, and while still in combat type **`/os`**, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button. Each must print *"As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat."* and the panel must **not** open. Kill the mob and stand still: the panel must not open by itself once combat ends. Failure is the panel opening, silence with no message, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Open Sesame.

**14.** Hover the mini-map button and read its three keybind lines, then try each. **Left-Click** must toggle Auto-Opening, **Right-Click** must toggle Speedy Loot, and **Middle-Click** must toggle Loot Sound. After each click the tooltip must redraw in place with that feature's state flipped between **Enabled** and **Disabled**. The button's icon must follow Auto-Opening: green bag on, red bag off, black bag paused. Failure is a click doing something other than what its line says, a state that doesn't update until you move the cursor away, or an icon that disagrees with the tooltip.

**15.** With Auto-Opening on, put several clams in your bags. They must open one after another with no clicks from you, over a second or two. Now fill your bags until you have **fewer than four free slots**. Chat must read *"Auto-Opening is paused until you have at least 4 empty bag slots."*, the mini-map icon must go black, and nothing more may open. Free a slot: chat must read *"Auto-Opening has resumed."* and any clams still waiting must open. Failure is clams sitting unopened with slots to spare, clams opening while paused, or a pause message that never resolves into a resume.

**16.** On your Rogue, pickpocket or loot a fresh locked box. Chat must read *"[Box] will be automatically opened once it's unlocked."*, with the box as a clickable link. On a non-Rogue this line is deliberately silent unless **Lockboxes → Show for** is set to For All Characters. Hover the box. On **Classic Era and TBC Anniversary** there must be a block headed **Open Sesame** below the game's own lines, carrying a Lockpicking number that is **green when your skill clears the box and red when it doesn't**. Which line you get depends on the client: where the game already prints its own *Requires Lockpicking* line, the block shows *Your Lockpicking* with your rank instead of repeating it, so expect those two flavors to differ. WoW Forever was covered in step 6. Hover the mini-map button: a **Locked Items** heading must list the box, with ` x2` beside it if you carry two of the same. Now pick the lock. The box must open itself within a second or so, with no second click, and its row must be gone from Locked Items. Failure is no chat line, no block on a Rogue who plainly has the skill, both lines at once, a colour that disagrees with the numbers, or a box that sits unopened after it's unlocked.

**17.** Left-click the mini-map button to turn **Auto-Opening off**, put a clam in your bags, and talk to a vendor. With the vendor window open, left-click the button to turn Auto-Opening back **on**. The clam must stay shut. Close the vendor window: the clam must open on its own within a second or two. On your Rogue, repeat it with **Stealth** instead of the vendor. The clam must stay shut while you're stealthed and open within a second or two of leaving Stealth. Failure is a clam opening while the vendor window is up or while you're stealthed, which would break your Stealth, or a clam that stays shut once you're clear.

**18.** With Speedy Loot on and plenty of bag room, loot a corpse. The loot window must **never appear at all**, not even a flash, and everything, coin included, must land in your bags. Now fill your bags completely and loot a corpse holding real items: the loot window must **stay up** with those items still in it, so you can take them by hand. Failure is a loot window flashing for half a second on every corpse, or a window hidden while loot is still stranded in it.

**19.** Pick something common you can farm, such as cloth or a grey drop. Paste its item link or item ID into the **Add Item** box on the **Ignore List** panel and press Enter. It must appear as a row with its icon straight away. Kill something that drops it: Speedy Loot must **leave it in the loot window**, and chat must read *"[Item] was left in the loot window for you to open yourself."* Wait at least five seconds, then take it by hand: chat must now read *"[Item] is on your Ignore List, so Auto-Opening left it alone."* Next, with Auto-Opening off, put a clam in your bags, add the clam to the list too, and turn Auto-Opening back on. The clam must stay **shut**. Finally, click **Restore Defaults** and confirm the prompt. Both of your additions must be gone, and the clam must open on its own within a second or two. Failure is an ignored item looted anyway or left behind with no explanation, a clam opening while it's on the list, or a Restore Defaults that fires with no confirmation or leaves your additions behind.

**20.** With Loot Toasts on, kill something and loot it. Rows must appear where you put the panel in step 1, or above the centre of the screen if you didn't move it. Each row shows the item's own icon, its name in its quality colour, and ` x2` after a stack of more than one, and fades away on its own after about five seconds. Coin must get its own row, with a coin icon and an amount reading like *"1g 24s 07c"*. An Uncommon (green) or better off a corpse must also play a single chime. Type `/reload`: the introduction panel must **not** come back, and the next toast must land in the same place. Then open **Notifications**, scroll to Loot Toasts and click **Reset Position**: the rows must return to above the centre of the screen. Failure is rows drawing somewhere you didn't put them, rows that never fade, no row for coin, an item name arriving as a bare number, no chime on a green off a corpse, or the introduction reappearing after a reload.

**21.** Read back the add-on's chat lines you've now seen: the pause and resume lines (step 15), the box waiting to be unlocked (step 16), and the item left in the loot window and the Ignore List line (step 19). Every one must be branded **Open Sesame //**, read as one complete sentence, and carry a **clickable item link** where an item is named. The count in the pause line must read as a real number. Nothing this add-on prints is ever sent to another player, so none of it may show up in any chat channel. Failure is `nil` or a stray `%s` or `%d` in a line, a raw `|cff...|Hitem:` sequence showing as text instead of a link, or the same line repeating over and over as your bags update.

**22.** Type `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools**. Only the warning paragraph and the **Enable Diagnostic Tools** toggle may be visible. The toggle must be **off**, even though you left it on earlier, because diagnostics never carries over a reload. Tick it: seventeen sections must appear without reopening the panel, in this order: Event Log, Event Registration, API Endpoints, Loot Method, Other Add-ons, Relevant CVars, Display Context, Saved Variables, Player & Spells, Locked Boxes, Library Versions, four Validate Data sections, Taint Log, and External Tools. Click **Start Event Log**. Fill your bags and loot something so the game tells you your inventory is full. Then press an ability that's on cooldown a dozen times, so the game prints red errors that have nothing to do with looting. Come back and click **Show Captured Events**. The inventory-full firing must appear as a **full timestamped line**. The cooldown errors must **not** appear as individual lines. Instead they must be one counted row in the block at the end headed **"Suppressed uncorrelated traffic:"**, in the shape `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE(56, Ability is not ready yet.) x12`. Click each remaining report button in turn: every one must fill its box with readable text rather than an error or a blank, and **Other Add-ons** must list Open-Sesame ending in `[loadable, loaded]`. Untick the toggle: everything below it must vanish at once. Failure is the toggle already on after a reload, cooldown spam flooding the log as individual lines, the inventory-full firing swallowed into the summary, or a report box left blank.

**23.** *Optional, and only worth running on a non-English client.* Log in on one and open every panel. Every label, description, note and mouseover tooltip must render in that language. Failure is a raw key like `OPTIONS_ENABLE_LOOT_TOASTS` or `LOCKBOXES_DESCRIPTION` showing on screen instead of words. Then trigger the pause message and an item message: the number and the item name must land inside sensible sentences, with no `nil` and no leftover `%s` or `%d`. Loot coin and read the money toast: the amounts must carry that client's own gold, silver and copper symbols. Finally, on a Rogue with Lockpicking trained on Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, click **Read Player & Spells** in Diagnostic Tools: the last line must give your rank as a **number**. Then hover a locked box: the Open Sesame block must be there, its number green or red. **This last check is the highest-risk one here.** The add-on finds your rank by matching the skill's name exactly as that client spells it, and when the match fails, there is no error to warn you. The number silently turns white, or on the flavor that prints its own requirement line, the block never appears at all.

When every step passes on Classic Era, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
